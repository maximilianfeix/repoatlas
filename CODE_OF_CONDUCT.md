# Code of Conduct

RepoAtlas welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone taking part in the project is expected to help keep its community respectful, inclusive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful when discussing code, ideas, and decisions.
- Offer feedback that is specific and constructive; accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and points of view.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm caused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention, trolling, and sharing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable. Critique ideas and implementation without targeting people.

## Scope and enforcement

These standards apply to RepoAtlas issues, pull requests, and other project spaces, as well as when someone represents the project publicly. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, and may warn, restrict, or exclude participants depending on the impact and circumstances.

Report a concern privately to [contact@aquaris.dev](mailto:contact@aquaris.dev). Include a link or enough context to identify the incident. Reports will be handled discreetly.

This code is adapted for RepoAtlas from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
